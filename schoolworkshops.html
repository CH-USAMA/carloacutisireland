Are you interested in a school visit or workshop?

We organise interactive, fun workshops on Carlo Acutis for young people. Here is all you need to do.

SchoolsParishesRetreats

Four steps

All you need to do

01

Tell your principal or teacher

Let them know you would like to host a visit or workshop in your school, parish or retreat.

02

Decide what you would like included

Pick from what we offer, below — or tell us your own idea. We are very happy to take on any suggestions you might have for the visit.

03

Write to us

Contact us on info@carloacutisireland.org and a member of our Carlo team will be in touch.

04

We come to you

The directors of Carlo Acutis Youth Ireland volunteer their time to run the visit or workshop, on a date and time arranged with you.

What a workshop can include

  • Interactive workshops on Carlo
  • Quiz events
  • The Eucharistic miracles exhibition
  • The life of Carlo
  • Q&A sessions

And whatever you would like to add. We are very happy to take on any suggestions you might have.

Invite us to your school

Tell us who you are and what you would like, and a member of the Carlo team will be in touch to arrange a date.

info@carloacutisireland.org →
